The Daymap connector is deployed.

Add it to Claude:
  1. In Claude (web, desktop or mobile), open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Use this URL: {{ .Values.publicUrl | trimSuffix "/" }}/mcp
  3. Click Connect, then sign in with your own Daymap username and password.
     Allowed Daymap usernames: {{ join ", " .Values.connector.allowedDaymapUsers }}

Check it's up:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "daymap-connector.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  curl http://localhost:8080/healthz
